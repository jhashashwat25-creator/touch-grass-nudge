Ten minutes. Outside. No phone.

Tell it how much time you have and where you are. A small open-source AI model, running inside this browser tab, writes you one tiny mission.

How long?

Runs fully in your browser with Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (Apache-2.0) through Transformers.js. Nothing you pick is sent to a server. The first run downloads the model (a few hundred MB), then your browser caches it. Source on GitHub.
